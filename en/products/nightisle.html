02 — Features

What it does

Reads sleep stages, heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, respiration and wrist temperature from HealthKit, rebuilds the night's timeline and turns it into stages, readiness and advice you can act on. The dark interface is made for 3 a.m. — no glare.

Stage inference

Uses the time windows and whole-night statistics to re-cut awake, light, deep and REM.

Readiness score

Duration 40, quality 30, deep sleep 20, heart-rate dip 10 — the weighting is documented, not a black box.

Smart alarm

Wakes you at a lighter moment inside your window, reducing the odds of being pulled out of deep sleep.

Data export

Export whole nights and trends for a doctor's visit or your own long-term record.

Data & privacy

Health data is read from HealthKit and computed on device. No account, no upload. Privacy →
