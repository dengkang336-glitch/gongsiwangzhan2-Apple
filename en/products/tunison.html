02 — Features

What it does

One AVAudioEngine graph powers three tools: a chromatic tuner, a metronome with count-in and a sustained reference tone. The practice log turns itself into a list of weak spots, so the next session starts where it should.

Chromatic tuning

Target locking, cents readout and a steadiness indicator that holds up in a noisy room.

Metronome and count-in

Accents, subdivisions, tempo ladders and count-in — speed up a difficult bar one step at a time.

Drone

Sustains a note or interval for intonation and resonance work.

Practice log

Every session is logged, and weak spots are ranked by frequency into the next drill.

Data & privacy

Practice history is stored locally. No account and no uploaded statistics. Privacy →
