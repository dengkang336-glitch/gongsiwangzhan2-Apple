02 — Features

What it does

Walk in, find your own stone, leave one offering, close the page. A courtyard holds 96 independent stones; when it fills, a new courtyard grows. Offerings cannot be bought, one per day, with no counters or rankings.

96 stones per courtyard

Each stone has its own number. When a courtyard fills, a new one opens and the old one stays as it was.

One offering a day

No purchases, no stacking, no counts or rankings — one gesture, expressed once.

Reviewed before public

Public entries are reviewed before they appear; private entries do not exist in the public view.

Seven languages

Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French and German, switchable from the entry.

Data & privacy

Accounts, inscriptions and photos are stored server-side. No location or contacts access, and no recommendations. Privacy →
