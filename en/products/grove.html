02 — Features

What it does

Walk slowly between lawns and stones. Raise a stone for someone you lost, leave a light, and meet others doing the same — with no channel to talk to them. One codebase for desktop and mobile, nine languages, installable as a PWA.

Walk freely

Third-person camera with keyboard, mouse, touch and an on-screen stick — tap the ground and you walk there.

Stones and flowers

Choose a form, a stone colour and an inscription, then leave flowers or a light. Offerings cannot be bought.

No chat channel

You can see one another, but there is no DM, comment or friend request. The quiet is part of the product.

Nine languages

Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German and Russian, across UI and scene copy.

Data & privacy

Inscriptions and photos are stored server-side. Public content is reviewed before it appears; private entries do not exist in the public view. Privacy →
