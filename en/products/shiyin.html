02 — Features

What it does

Phoneme fragments in 20 languages plus a bed of filtered noise, played back at random intervals. Layer several banks, add delay and reverb, and record the whole session. Every sample is synthesised in-house — no third-party audio.

21 sound banks

Twenty languages — Chinese, reversed Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi and more — plus noise: 1,301 fragments in total.

Layered banks

Each bank has its own level and silence gap, so layering adds density rather than noise.

Effect chain

Delay and reverb sit on the main mix, with a level meter driven by live RMS.

Session recording

Records the session to m4a for playback, sharing or deletion, with lock-screen control.

Data & privacy

No network requests, no account, no analytics. The microphone opens only when you start recording. Privacy →
