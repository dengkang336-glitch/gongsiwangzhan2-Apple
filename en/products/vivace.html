02 — Features

What it does

Vivace reads the heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen and sleep data already in HealthKit and turns it into a health brief you can actually read: 30 metrics, 21-day trends, sleep stages, workout heart-rate curves and an A4 report you can hand to a doctor. Everything stays on device.

30 health metrics

Resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, respiration, wrist temperature, steps and training load, aggregated by day, week and month.

21-day trends

Every metric carries a baseline band, so you can see how far today sits from your normal — not just an isolated number.

Sleep stages and recovery

Re-slices the night by time window and combines resting heart rate, HRV and sleep into one recovery score.

Medical report export

Generates a Chinese A4 PDF or CSV in one tap, with metric tables that paginate cleanly for a clinic visit.

Data & privacy

Health data is processed on device only. No upload, no account, no advertising profiles. Privacy →
