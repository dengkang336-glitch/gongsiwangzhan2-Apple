03 — Company

About Yunshu Ark

Good software is finished, not merely imagined.

Yunshu Ark (Guangzhou Yunshu Ark Technology Co., Ltd.) is a software company based in Guangzhou. We design, build and operate our own mobile and web / 3D products, and take on custom development from prototype to launch.

We choose our own product direction: quiet tools that get opened every day — health, scanning, fill light, practice, remembrance. They do not rely on notifications or streaks to bring people back; they rely on being there the next time they are needed.

A few engineering habits are fixed. Decide the direction before building. Make a prototype you can tap before writing the interface. Every product ships with a reproducible build, tests and a self-check script. Launch is not the end; every update goes through the same pipeline.

Privacy is not a slogan. If something can be computed on the device, it is not uploaded; if it does not need to be collected, it is not collected. Products with accounts and servers (Memorial Grove, Lampside) spell out what is stored, where, and how to delete it.

We do not buy data, sell data, use dark-pattern subscriptions, or invent download counts and ratings. Every product shown on this site has a real codebase and a real interface behind it.

Method

How we work

Four habits, from choosing a direction to every change after launch.

01

Direction first

Every new piece starts with three real directions. Nothing gets built until one is chosen. Speed does not help if the direction is wrong.

02

Prototype first

A prototype you can tap exposes problems faster than a requirements document. Interfaces are validated on a real device, not in imagination.

03

Tests first

Core logic has unit tests and key flows have end-to-end self-checks. Every change re-runs the same suite.

04

Fewer, finished

Better to ship one feature less than to leave a half-finished entry point. What is there has to be trustworthy.
