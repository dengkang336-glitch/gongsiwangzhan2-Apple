05 — Support

Support

Start with the questions below. If the answer is not there, write to us with your device model, OS version and a screenshot — it makes things much faster.

Where do I download the products?

Mobile apps are distributed through the App Store; web products open directly in a browser. Unreleased products are marked on their page. Everything on this site is currently in in-house development or beta; download links will be added here once released.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases made through the App Store are refunded by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot process the refund for you, but we can help explain the situation. Services bought elsewhere follow the terms shown at purchase.

Do you collect my health data?

No. Health data read by Vivace and NightIsle is computed on the device only — never uploaded, no account required. You can revoke access at any time in the system Health app.

How do I delete my account and data?

Memorial Grove and Lampside use accounts. Email us from the registered address, name the account, and we will delete it and confirm within seven business days.

Found a bug or have a suggestion?

Email our support address with the product name, device model, OS version and steps to reproduce. A screenshot helps. We usually reply within two business days.

Do you take custom work?

Yes. Send the goal, scope, timeframe and a budget range. We will first say whether it is a good fit, then provide a written quote and schedule.
